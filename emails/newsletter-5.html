Weeks have passed since sidequest, and honestly it has taken this long to get back to base level. We want to explain what happened and why sidequest did not take place in the originally planned location.

sidequest was planned to be held at an open air location in Schönefeld. What you didn't see was the year behind it. Around 50 people, most of whom had never worked together before, spent countless planning sessions and days of production on site building something new and special from scratch.

We took every step we could to make sure everything was in order, from planning and precautions to consultations and checking the permits we were given. What we didn't know was that some of those permits didn't hold up: they only covered a previous event, not the location itself. This came to light when the Ordnungsamt came to inspect. Going ahead would have meant heavy fines, a risk an entirely self-funded project like ours couldn't take, so we packed up immediately.

Rather than giving up, the team started looking for alternatives right away. The next few hours were countless phone calls, exhausting every contact and idea. With 19 hours until we were meant to open gates, Kreuzwerk made the most sense, all while the entire build in Schönefeld had to be torn down and moved, a huge logistical effort in itself. Big props to QS1 for the heavy lifting and logistics, and to the team at Kreuzwerk for making this possible and for putting up with our incessant questions and insane ideas.

We tried our best to bring small pieces of the original idea to the new location: wildflowers foraged from Kiekebusch, the original roofing from the stage, glass art installations, a hand-sewn sidequest lantern, and all the cozy bits from the ambient space. We even brought in our own lights, which is no easy feat to rig and design in a couple of hours (big props to Ella and the team).

We cannot express how grateful we are that so many of you still trusted our vision and came along. We really hope you got even a taste of what we had planned for you. A further thanks to the incredible team and all the artists for being onboard for whatever ride we proposed.

A small gallery from the build up and the event is now online — have a look.

This is definitely not the last you'll be hearing from us, but until then, happy sidequesting.
